> Total: **~160 issues** | **~50 critical** | **~85 important** | **~25 medium/low**

## Executive Summary

**UltraThink's core systems are fundamentally non-functional.** The three most devastating findings:

1. **All 13 hook symlinks are broken** — The entire lifecycle (memory recall, skill injection, privacy filtering, Tekiō wheel, code-intel checks) is completely dead. Nothing runs.
2. **Skill matching has never worked** — `raw.skills.map()` crashes because the registry is a flat object, not `{skills:[]}`. Even if hooks ran, zero skills would be injected.
3. **Tekiō `learning` category violates a DB CHECK constraint** — `wheelLearn()` always throws. The adaptive learning wheel has never stored a learning adaptation.

Combined effect: **The system is a ~52,000 token-per-session overhead that produces zero functional value.** The CLAUDE.md describes capabilities that have never worked in production.
